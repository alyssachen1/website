research associate intern @ fidelity digital asset management

summer 2025

i was the first intern to be recruited on the digital assets research team at fidelity investments, a small five-person team at the intersection of traditional finance and emerging digital asset markets.

my work centered on researching quantitative methodologies to for portfolio index maintenance and rebalancing and training machine learning models to forecast staking yields and revenue generation streams for our yield products on solana and ethereum.

on the fundamental research side, i developed an investment & risk evaluation framework for decentralized physical infrastructure projects (depin), which led to the hire of a depin analyst and helped establish our approach to assessing opportunities in this emerging sector. i also led a research review on the first depin project we evaluated, helium.
